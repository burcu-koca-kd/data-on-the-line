Data on the Line: Turning Demand Insights into Balanced Production · Case study · KoçDigital

1. Read Case_Text.pdf (about 10 minutes).
2. Open Case_Notebook.ipynb in Colab with the link or QR code you were given; the data files are downloaded automatically.
   You can also work locally with Jupyter. If you do, download this folder and keep the notebook and the data folder together.
3. Type your team number into the TEAM line at the top of the notebook (for example TEAM = "Team03"). Your files are named after it.
4. Don't try to open release_45_min.zip and release_95_min.zip yourself. The passwords are announced in the room and the notebook opens the files.
5. By minute 45: upload TeamXX_forecast.csv to the “Minute-45 forecast” form (Data on the Line – Minute-45 forecast); that is the forecast that gets scored.
6. By minute 115: upload three files to the “Final submission” form (Data on the Line – Final submission): TeamXX_submission.zip, TeamXX_slides.pptx (your cover and three slides, made with Slide_Template.pptx) and TeamXX_notebook.ipynb. Nothing is accepted after the deadline.
   Don't leave the slides until the end: fill them in alongside the analysis from Part 2 of the notebook (production plan) onwards.

The data, tables and checks are ready; the models are yours to build.
